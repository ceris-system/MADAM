Privacy policy

Mama Sita's Apps Dashboard

Effective date: November 28, 2026

What this dashboard does

This browser-based dashboard connects to Google Sheets so you can view and, where your Google account has permission, add, edit, or delete rows in spreadsheet sources that you choose.

Google account and spreadsheet access

When you connect, Google Identity Services asks you to authorize the dashboard. The current version requests the Google Sheets scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets, which permits read and write access to spreadsheets available to your Google account. The dashboard sends Google Sheets API requests only for spreadsheet sources configured in the dashboard. Google account and spreadsheet permissions continue to apply.

Spreadsheet contents are fetched and displayed in your browser and are sent directly between your browser and Google's services. The dashboard operator does not run a server that stores spreadsheet contents.

Information stored on your device

The dashboard stores the OAuth client ID you enter, spreadsheet category names and URLs, selected categories, and sheet names in your browser's local storage. This information stays in that browser until you remove a source or clear the site's stored data. It does not automatically sync to other devices.

The Google access token is kept in memory while the page is open and is not saved by the dashboard in local storage. The service worker caches static app files for offline loading; it does not cache spreadsheet contents or access tokens. Connecting to Google Sheets still requires an internet connection.

Sharing, removal, and revoking access

The dashboard does not send spreadsheet contents to the dashboard operator or another dashboard service. People who have access to the same spreadsheet can see or change it according to its Google sharing settings. Removing a source from the dashboard removes its saved URL from your browser only; it does not delete the spreadsheet or its data from Google Drive.

You can revoke the dashboard's Google access in your Google Account security settings. You can also clear the dashboard's local storage using your browser's site-data settings.

Third-party services

Authentication and spreadsheet requests use Google services, and the dashboard is hosted on GitHub Pages. Those providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.
